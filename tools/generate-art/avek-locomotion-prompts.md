# Avek locomotion atlas prompts

Canonical reference: JAMVERSE '96 specification, K.22, plus the existing Amp City captain portrait and gameplay cells. Avek is an aggressive, commanding male metal deity / heavy-rock icon. Preserve the mask/visor-obscured face; natural skin, eye and hair colors are not canonically established and are intentionally not exposed in these new sprites. Metallic armor, leather, molten orange/gold/white details and electric energy seams define the silhouette. His guitar is already visible in the portrait and stays safely stowed during court movement, so these locomotion sprites omit it. The basketball is rendered as a separate game object. This character has an approved mirror-safe body policy because the visor and stowed guitar have no fixed side.

All right-facing source sheets are imagegen-created and reviewed at full resolution. Source cells that touched/crossed a boundary were excluded. The packing process only isolates existing complete body components, scales uniformly, and losslessly packs them; it does not generate or deform poses.

- **idle:** six subtle idle poses in a 3x2 grid, `/workspace/generated_images/exec-816c16bf-7bb4-49eb-b036-2eb2b34e562f.png`. The first frontal-source candidate `/workspace/generated_images/exec-b29e44f9-37de-411d-8217-35b1b563f0cb.png` is rejected; only the profile-oriented replacement is used.
- **idle-ball:** four ready stances in a 2x2 grid, `/workspace/generated_images/exec-1c0c78eb-06fa-4b04-8b13-e72df9142eb3.png`.
- **dribble:** ten unique frames; seven safe cells from `/workspace/generated_images/exec-cddf5b3a-f177-4a57-8235-c49c535f1b9e.png` plus three distinct idle-ball stances. Source dribble cells 6, 7 and 9 are excluded because their alpha silhouettes touch the panel edge.
- **run:** eleven alternating strides in a 4x3 grid, bottom-right cell blank, `/workspace/generated_images/exec-ae9c9823-2e2e-4b43-9ab6-3a4b1b6067b5.png`.
- **dribble-run:** five safe low-hand running poses from `/workspace/generated_images/exec-66b35cbe-2f18-4690-8378-854207d47f4d.png`, interleaved with six unique run-cycle poses. Source dribble-run cells 2, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 10 are excluded due to edge contact.

Each atlas is normalized to a 72px pose height, baseline 94 in 96px cells, and losslessly encoded as WebP. Exact source hashes, chosen indices, crop coordinates and transformed pixel hashes are recorded in `avek-locomotion-layouts.json`.
- **Dribble continuation:** a supplemental transparent 2x2 sheet, `/workspace/generated_images/exec-c24b0b30-b4f9-48a8-8756-dbd83ffc0964.png`, was generated from the accepted Avek idle-ball atlas as an identity/style reference. Cells 1, 2, 3 fill the low dribble and rising phases; cell 0 closes the loop in a ready stance. This replaces three borrowed ready poses, keeping the full ten-frame stationary cycle in dribble motion. The visor remains opaque, copper lightning is clearly readable, and the guitar is absent from court movement.
