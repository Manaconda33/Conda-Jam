# Kraken dunk-tomahawk attempt 04 gameplay review

**Verdict: do not promote; attempt 05 corrects the remaining frame 9 offset.**

The rerun report contains 60 captures for both facings and both zooms, with no uncaught browser errors and correct two-point results. The frame 6–8 gathers are in contact range. Frame 9 is still too high and slightly too far forward relative to the extended hand in both mirrored views: the ball approaches the backboard area while the hand sits lower and behind it. This is a track calibration miss, not an event/timing failure. The run is candidate-injected and does not establish production-loader integration.

Attempt 05 lowers frame 9 height from 1.3 to 0.95 and forward offset from 12 to 8. Its 120% captures bring the ball back to the extended hand. See the independent attempt 05 gameplay review for that verdict.
