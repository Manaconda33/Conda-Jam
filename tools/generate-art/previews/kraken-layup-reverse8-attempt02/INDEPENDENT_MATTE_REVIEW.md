# Independent matte review — Kraken layup-reverse attempt02

Reviewed on 2026-10-28 from the v1/v2 light and dark contact sheets and both layout reports. Scope: matte edge quality, anatomy/identity at runtime scale, cell separation, and readiness for track work.

## Verdict: FAIL — retain as cleanup experiments only

The eight frames are distinct and Kraken's auburn hair, pointed ear, copper amulet, and burgundy/copper uniform remain recognizable. The layout reports eight unique frame hashes and consistent 80×96 cells. Those positives do not make these runtime candidates acceptable.

Both v1 and v2 leave dark brown background material attached to the figures as visible blocky halos and shadow slabs, especially around torsos and arms. The figures do not read as clean isolated transparent sprites against either light or dark backgrounds. There are also isolated pixels/fragments around poses 0, 5, and 6; pose 5 includes a detached fragment beside the lower-row figure. These are not merely subtle anti-alias fringes. At the preview scale they look like debris and undermine the outline.

The matte and nearest-neighbor reduction produce coarse noisy pixels from the source's painterly shading. The right-facing character identity is reasonably consistent, but the reverse-layup body/arm sequence is hard to parse at game scale because the matte artifacts obscure the silhouette. Cell content technically remains within the 80×96 cells; minimum reported margins are L/T/R/B = 8/21/9/2 px, leaving only 2 px below the lowest foot.

Do not proceed to ball-track calibration, packing, or registration from either matte. Preserve v1 and v2 as rejected cleanup attempts. Correct the background segmentation with a cleaner subject mask and inspect both full-size poses and runtime previews before another review.
