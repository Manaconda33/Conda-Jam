# Cove Knell — supplemental locomotion poses

## Candidate seven-pose prompt

Draw one single horizontal strip of exactly seven separate full-body sprite drawings, evenly spaced in seven columns. Use genuine transparent RGBA, no background, checkerboard, grid, floor, effects, labels, or props. Each figure is complete and isolated with broad transparent space; no touching or overlap. Keep consistent scale and crisp 1990s arcade basketball cel shading.

Match canonical Cove Knell: recognizable adult human man with deep dark-brown skin, medium-length deep-purple locs, thick dark beard and goatee, powerful natural human build. He wears a modest dark plum/burgundy Vesper Harbor uniform with copper-gold trim and white piping, black wristbands and dark shoes. All hands empty.

Left to right: relaxed upright ball-ready stance; another upright ball-ready stance with hands at waist; upright ready stance with one open hand forward; balanced low stationary dribble with hand reaching down; then three different low forward dribble-running strides with a reaching hand. No additional objects.

The rightmost figure in this candidate touches the image border and was excluded. The remaining six clean, separate drawings are read left to right; the first of these six was not needed and is omitted from the final atlas.

## Accepted mixed-action supplement prompt

Create a horizontal strip with exactly seven separate complete transparent-RGBA basketball-player sprites, seven equal columns and one row, ample gaps, and no background or glow. Match canonical Cove Knell: adult human man, deep dark-brown skin, thick medium-length purple locs, dark beard/goatee, modest Vesper Harbor plum uniform with copper-gold and white piping, black wristbands and dark high-tops. Crisp 1990s arcade cel sprite art. Every hand is empty. Do not include basketballs, circles, objects, labels, floor, checkerboard, shadows, borders, effects, or detached marks.

Left to right: two relaxed upright idle poses; one upright alert ball-ready stance; two different balanced stationary low dribble reaches; then two distinct low forward dribble-running strides with a reaching hand and alternating leg positions. Keep complete locs, hands and shoes in view, same identity and scale.

Only the third pose and the final two poses are assigned to the 42-frame atlas; the other four distinct figures remain unassigned source drawings.

## Rejected attempts

- A separate single-pose generation and its background-removal edit retained an opaque radial gradient; neither image was used.
- A seven-pose dribble-run sheet baked basketballs into the hands. It was rejected because the game renders its own ball; none of those bodies were packed.
