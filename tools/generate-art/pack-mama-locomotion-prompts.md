# Pack Mama locomotion sprite production

Canon K.33: teal-haired protective human woman with the identifying emerald wolf-eared hood, a rich green athletic adaptation of her storybook gown, warmth and pack-leader confidence. The character remains fully human. No wolf muzzle, fur body, paws or tail are allowed. The hood ears remain visible in every clip. Current team art uses the hood and flowing teal hair; for small basketball sprites the hair is gathered into a compact braid so it does not cross cell boundaries.

The main source `/workspace/generated_images/exec-f0aa0fed-70ea-4379-b00e-17b749e6dcbe.png` contains35 distinct complete figures in a clear 7x5 sheet. It is mapped as six idle frames, four ready frames, ten dribble frames, seven run frames and seven dribble-run frames. A generated 4x2 supplemental sheet `/workspace/generated_images/exec-f090f611-d611-4375-985c-861c9bd795f3.png` supplies four run strides and four low-hand dribble-run strides. The composite uses34 of the main source figures (the seventh extra idle is deliberately excluded) plus all eight unique supplements, yielding exactly42 authored poses at the requested counts.

Two other imagegen candidates are excluded: the large-haired source `/workspace/generated_images/exec-0fae9785-a627-4c3d-a42e-d2bbc24b01b3.png` joined hair across cells; the imagegen resize `/workspace/generated_images/exec-fbb5570e-991b-4290-85e3-b672f4c83bd0.png` returned only35 figures, so it could not close the 42-pose requirement.

The source compositor and exact per-frame source map are `assemble-pack-mama-source.py` and `pack-mama-locomotion-source-map.json`. Component isolation assigns existing alpha pixels to their nearest figure before transparent 300px cells are assembled. Supplemental running poses are uniformly reduced to match the height of the corresponding source action. Nothing is mirrored, duplicated, interpolated, or drawn by the packer. The generic 42-pose packer emits the five action atlases and records decoded source hashes. Canon-approved body mirroring remains a separate policy.
