Create one transparent RGBA 7-column × 6-row sheet with EXACTLY42 separate full-body drawings of canonical athlete Marin Dusk. Use the portrait as face and hair identity reference and the gameplay sprite as costume/style reference. Marin is the same adult human woman in every pose: light warm-olive skin, athletic natural adult human build, the same face and long thick dark-purple hair gathered back with a fine gold headband. Preserve her human ears and her distinctive purple hair/headband. She wears a Vesper Harbor Undertow dark burgundy/plum basketball uniform with copper-gold trim and clean white piping, matching wristbands, and dark high-top basketball shoes. Keep the kit modest and full-coverage; no robe or cape.

Use polished 1990s arcade basketball sprite art with crisp cel shading, bold clean contours and readable anatomy. Maintain consistent camera and scale. True RGBA alpha transparency only; clean antialiased edges with no fringe. Center each entire athlete in an individual cell and leave broad transparent gutters and an outer margin. Keep every strand of hair, hand, shoe and limb inside the cell; no adjacent figures touch or connect. Draw each figure at about60% of cell height with clear vertical row gaps. No floor, shadows, scenery, text, numbers, logos, borders, checkerboard, basketball or props. Hands are empty for the game-rendered ball.

The 42 cells read left-to-right, top-to-bottom: idle6; idle-ball4; ten distinct low stationary dribbles with a reaching hand; eleven different running strides with alternating legs and arm swing; eleven low forward dribble-run strides, reaching toward the court. Exact sequence layout: row1 idle1–6 and idle-ball1; row2 idle-ball2–4 and dribble1–4; row3 dribble5–10 and run1; row4 run2–8; row5 run9–11 and dribble-run1–4; row6 dribble-run5–11. Exactly seven separate complete figures per row, exactly six rows, all poses different. Preserve Marin's recognizable adult face, long purple hair and Vesper Harbor colors without adding fantasy anatomy or accessories.
