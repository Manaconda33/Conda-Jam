# J.122 pose04 canonical wording correction

The original verbatim `pose-04-prompt.md` is preserved for provenance. Its description “lowest point” is not canonical for pose04. The accepted J.105 scythe sequence defines pose04 as the low rearward sweep, rising into the jump; pose05 is the deepest point, with a more forward torso lean and the carry hand lower near knee level.

Independent review of the pose04 candidate confirms it reads as the canonical pose04 low rear sweep: the image-left/trailing carry hand reaches behind the hip around hip/upper-thigh level, with a modest torso pitch. Record it as `low-rear-sweep`, never as the deepest point. Pose05 must own the lower hand/deepest-sweep description.
