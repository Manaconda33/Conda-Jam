# Art direction

The visual target is saturated late-arcade cabinet art: violet audience wall, warm hardwood, gold rules, teal/orange scoreboard accents, nearest-neighbor scaling, and high contrast. The internal viewport is 480×360. Floor projection uses world x/y/z, the court paints behind athletes, feet/shadows use ground coordinates, and z offsets the sprite and ball visually.

`public/assets/captain-portrait-atlas.png` is an original generated 7×5 RGBA portrait sheet, one tile per canon captain in manifest order. It includes signature identity details and is used in the title cards and 35-team selector. `public/assets/team-crest-atlas.png` adds 35 original mascot crests alongside each portrait. The single-file build inlines both assets; the normal static build copies them to `dist/public/assets/`.

Gameplay silhouettes still draw procedurally rather than from a generated animation atlas. Canonical nonhuman rigs have dedicated anatomy branches for the kit fox (four-footed, pointed ears, muzzle/tail), dragon (wings, four limbs, gold flecks, ceremonial cloth and long tail), cobra (expanded hood, markings, face mask and coils), wombat (barrel body, short legs and lantern), jaguar (feline muzzle, spots and flag jersey), zebra-orangutan hybrid (striped orange body with extra-long arms and hands), and android (noir torso and a blank screen beneath a fedora). Humans and fantasy humanoids use character-specific hair, glasses, horns, ears, wings, hat, antlers, or other feature tags where implemented. Supporting-athlete portraits and animation frame recipes remain incomplete.

The athlete shapes are intentionally original and built from local Canvas primitives. Humanoid athletes have drawn limbs, running gait, block/shoot/dunk arm poses, a brief directional pass pose, and a timed raised-arm scoring celebration; the zebra-orangutan's long arms exaggerate the celebration. Stature-based scaling and canonical animal silhouettes remain. Visual review exposed and corrected the Wombat rig's missing full body, improved long-arm zebra framing, strengthened Dragon Queen/Suga anatomy cues, and added a noir body/fedora to Alexithymia. They do not yet meet the requested sprite-sheet frame counts, pixel-dither detail, portrait set, complete signature animation set, or final art fidelity bar. Screenshots in `artifacts/screenshots/` are actual browser captures, not mockups.
