# Art direction

The visual target is saturated late-arcade cabinet art: violet audience wall, warm hardwood, gold rules, teal/orange scoreboard accents, nearest-neighbor scaling, and high contrast. The internal viewport is 480×360. Floor projection uses world x/y/z, the court paints behind athletes, feet/shadows use ground coordinates, and z offsets the sprite and ball visually.

`public/assets/captain-portrait-atlas.png` is an original generated 7×5 RGBA portrait sheet, one tile per canon captain in manifest order. It includes signature identity details and is used in the title cards and 35-team selector. `public/assets/team-crest-atlas.png` adds 35 original mascot crests alongside each portrait. `captain-gameplay-sprites.png` adds a 7×5 full-body sprite atlas for the 35 captains; `supporting-player-gameplay-sprites.png` adds a 10×7 atlas for the other 70 athletes. Both atlases retain transparent backgrounds and are mapped in team-manifest order into live on-court rendering. Sprites are scaled by stature and court depth, mirror for direction, bob with motion, rise with simulation z, and retain the existing knockdown, ball, marker, and name overlays. The standalone build inlines all four atlases; the static build copies them to `dist/public/assets/`.

Each player now has a distinct generated on-court sprite, but the art is a single action pose rather than the complete multi-frame animation atlas specified in section H. Canonical nonhuman rigs have dedicated anatomy branches for the kit fox (four-footed, pointed ears, muzzle/tail), dragon (wings, four limbs, gold flecks, ceremonial cloth and long tail), cobra (expanded hood, markings, face mask and coils), wombat (barrel body, short legs and lantern), jaguar (feline muzzle, spots and flag jersey), zebra-orangutan hybrid (striped orange body with extra-long arms and hands), and android (noir torso and a blank screen beneath a fedora). Humans and fantasy humanoids use character-specific hair, glasses, horns, ears, wings, hat, antlers, or other feature tags where implemented. Supporting-athlete portraits and animation frame recipes remain incomplete.

The gameplay sprite art is original, generated for this project and composed into the game without external requests. The existing procedural rigs remain as a loading fallback and for action-specific procedural effects. Humanoid athletes have drawn limbs, running gait, block/shoot/dunk arm poses, a brief directional pass pose, and a timed raised-arm scoring celebration; the zebra-orangutan's long arms exaggerate the celebration. Stature-based scaling and canonical animal silhouettes remain. Visual review exposed and corrected the Wombat rig's missing full body, improved long-arm zebra framing, strengthened Dragon Queen/Suga anatomy cues, and added a noir body/fedora to Alexithymia. The single-pose atlases do not yet meet the requested per-state frame counts, eight-direction run sheets, signature animation set, or final animation review bar. Screenshots in `artifacts/screenshots/` are actual browser captures, not mockups.
