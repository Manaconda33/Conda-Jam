# Assumptions

- The uploaded JAMVERSE master prompt is the complete source of canonical character identities and franchise naming. No Cleo entry is created.
- Two athletes per franchise are active at once. Starters select a partner before tipoff; during each between-period break either partner can rotate with the third roster athlete, with individual box-score totals preserved across stints.
- Shots score 2 inside the 34-world-unit arc radius and 3 beyond it, with value locked at release. A shot must descend across its target plane, but backboard and rim collision response remains a later feature.
- Regulation uses four configurable periods and overtime repeats on ties. Inbounds are instantaneous after a short score pause. Foul/free-throw rules are omitted as arcade-style simplifications.
- Each player's made streak resets when any teammate scores. An active fire streak survives a teammate's score and ends on an opponent's make or after four more makes by that athlete.
- The game is standalone and offline capable. There is no permission to deploy or push, and no external copyrighted art, recorded game audio, logos, teams, or likenesses are used.
- Where no canonical supporting-character art was supplied, support athletes use original names and game-only visual seed variation; their appearance is not canon lore.
