# Known issues

## P1 — Contact defense does not stop the ball

Pressing shoot/block on defense can deflect jump shots and dunk releases when the defender jumps close enough and at the right height. Descending-shot goaltending is called within an approximate protected hoop region; exact rim contact is not simulated. Turbo+pass body-checks off-ball opponents with strength-based knockdowns, and the CPU attempts occasional checks. There is no foul/charge system or knockdown line in the box score.

## P1 — Art and roster selection are early

The league manifest has 35 teams and all 105 named athletes. Menu selectors choose the active second athlete and quarter-break hotkeys rotate between partners and the third athlete with stats retained. Captain cards now depict the six key nonhuman species with custom bodies and draw selected captain features. Supporting athletes use generated visual-seed variations in skin, hair, uniform, and court gear, but the procedural Canvas art still lacks a full animation atlas, dedicated portraits for each supporting player, signature celebration props, and enough anatomical detail for final art acceptance.

## P1 — Tournament depth is limited

Season Run now persists an eight-opponent route, advances on wins, and ends the run on a loss. The separate league table records and ranks completed non-practice matches for all 35 franchises. There is no seeded tournament bracket or playoff progression.

## P2 — Arcade court collision/presentation gaps

Rim and backboard contact use approximate collision volumes rather than detailed geometry. The crowd uses repeated blocks and the camera does not smoothly scroll all the way to both baskets. The canvas is pixel-scaled and readable, but not yet a full NBA-era sprite composition.

## P2 — Controls/settings gaps

Keyboard movement/action remapping is supported and saved for P1/P2. Up to four gamepads can take all athlete slots, including a four-pad-only match; disconnecting a pad releases that athlete to CPU or keyboard control, and reconnects are reassigned. Third/fourth keyboard bindings and physical controller variants remain untested. Gamepad button remapping and separate sound-bus controls are not complete. Reduced-flash disables the crowd light twinkle; it does not limit every motion/animation effect.
