## Scope and evidence

Visually inspected all 35 cells of captain portrait, gameplay and first-run sheets and all 70 cells of supporting portrait and gameplay sheets using image viewing tools. Captain sheet order is the 7-column/5-row master roster order; supporting order is 10 columns/7 rows, partner then alternate per team. Source paths are recorded per athlete in `src/content/animation-mirror-policy.js`. Every roster ID has an explicit entry.

These are body-direction decisions based on written canon and existing reference art. They do not approve future generated clips, certify all existing art as canon-faithful, or establish that a human creator has approved it. Each new clip still needs visual quality and identity review. Recheck any new text, directional logo or newly supplied side-specific reference. Render jersey numbers and readable lettering separately with their correct orientation. Random hair parting, optional jewelry/props, a white sleeve or pose-specific ball hand do not establish fixed anatomy when canon assigns no side.

103 body designs permit mirroring under the supplied canon. E. Indigo requires separate left/right eye treatment. GB Design requires a correctly oriented Union Jack rather than a reflected flag. Alex has required two-color cheek circuitry and headset but no prescribed side assignment, so inventing a fixed cyan-left/magenta-right rule would exceed the master. The same reasoning applies to unspecified hairpin, tattoos, split hair and prop hands.

Existing art has separate fidelity defects, recorded below rather than hidden by mirror approval. Tiny eye details and accurate flag diagonals are not reliably certified at sprite-sheet scale; production direction crops must make those readable. Supporting portraits use several recurring face/hair templates and palette variants; review approval here only means no fixed side was found, and does not certify sufficient individuality or animation production completeness.
