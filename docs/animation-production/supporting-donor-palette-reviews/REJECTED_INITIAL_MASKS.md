# Rejected initial donor mask candidates

These retained `spot-check-*.png` sheets show the first fixed frame-relative mask attempt, before pose-specific fall exclusions and later luma/hue calibration. They are preserved as rejected QA outputs; the accepted profiles and current previews are indexed in each donor's `matrix.json` and `spot-check-seven-actions-9x7.png`.

- Juniper Vale: fall hair was recolored where the fixed shorts zone crossed the rear locks.
- Moxie Capwell: fall hair overlapped the torso zone in a few target palettes.
- Piper Quickstep: the broad orange-red threshold tinted face/arm pixels in fall poses.
- Dorian Mycel: the source jersey stayed purple in several poses because the fixed torso region missed the garment.
- Reed Ashcroft, Nova Stitch and Echo Catwalk: their initial spot samples did not show the defects found in the four families above; the current full matrices supersede them.

Source atlas names, hashes and selected frames are recorded in the manifest and matrices. These palette candidates are derived previews, not ImageGen output, and did not alter any source atlas or source-frame hash.
